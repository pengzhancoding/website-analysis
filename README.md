# Fast Passport Photo Online — Website Analysis

A detailed analysis and documentation of **[Fast Passport Photo Online](https://fastpassportphotoonline.com/)**, an AI-powered online passport and travel document photo service supporting 102+ countries and 207+ document templates.

---

## About the Service

[Fast Passport Photo Online](https://fastpassportphotoonline.com/) lets anyone create government-compliant passport and travel document photos at home using a smartphone or webcam — no photo studio visit required. Photos are automatically cropped, resized, and verified against official specifications for each country.

**Key advantages over traditional photo studios:**
- Flat $3.99 price vs. $15–$30 at studios
- Instant digital download + print-ready 4×6 sheets (6–9 photos per sheet)
- Refund guarantee if the issuing authority rejects the photo
- Works on mobile, tablet, and desktop
- Supports major pharmacy printers: Walgreens, CVS, Walmart, FedEx Office, Costco, Target, UPS Store

3. Download the compliant 2×2 inch JPEG and optional 4×6 print sheet with 6 photos
4. Print at any pharmacy kiosk or submit digitally

Photo tool for Canadian passport applications submitted to **Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC)**.

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|

| Digital size | 1,200 × 1,800 px at 300 DPI |
| Background | Plain white |

**Photographer signature requirement (unique to Canada):** One of the two required printed photos must have the following written on the back in non-smudging ink:
- Photographer's full name or studio name
- Photographer's address or studio address
- Date the photo was taken (YYYY-MM-DD format)

For self-taken photos, the applicant writes their own name, address, and capture date. Missing or improperly written photographer information is the dominant IRCC rejection pattern.

**Shared format with Brazil:** Canada and Brazil are the only countries using the 50 × 70 mm format — both predating ICAO Doc 9303. However, Brazil has no photographer-signature requirement, so a Brazilian photo cannot be directly converted to Canadian format without adding the back-of-print information.

### 11. Germany Passport Photos

**URL:** [https://fastpassportphotoonline.com/germany/passport](https://fastpassportphotoonline.com/germany/passport)

Photo tool for German passport applications, subject to **Bundesdruckerei** (Federal Printing Office) automated review — one of the strictest in Europe.

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Physical size | 35 mm × 45 mm (1.38 in × 1.77 in) |
| Digital size | 413 × 531 px at 300 DPI |

| Head coverage | 70–80% of photo height |
| Photo recency | Within 6 months |
| Glasses | Not permitted (2008 biometric format) |

**Bundesdruckerei review:** Checks 23 separate parameters. Most frequently failed: background quality — even slight texture, color gradients, or off-white tones are rejected. Other rejections: shadows, asymmetric head positioning >5°, hair covering eyebrows.

**Bürgeramt workflow:**
- Online appointment booking required (walk-ins unavailable in major cities)
- One printed biometric photo + national ID + €80 application fee
- In-house compliance check before acceptance
- Processing: 3–6 weeks (expedited: €110–€145)

Germany enforces the 2008 biometric photo format and the narrower 32–36 mm head-height band more strictly than most other Schengen members.

### 13. Singapore Passport Photos

**URL:** [https://fastpassportphotoonline.com/singapore/passport](https://fastpassportphotoonline.com/singapore/passport)

Photo tool for Singapore passport applications managed by the **Immigration and Checkpoints Authority (ICA)**.

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Physical size | 35 mm × 45 mm (1.38 in × 1.77 in) |
| Digital size | 400 × 514 px at 300 DPI |
| Background | Plain white |
| Head coverage | 70–80% of photo height |
| Max upload size | **60 KB** (JPEG — significantly stricter than most countries) |
| Photo recency | **Within 3 months** (stricter than the standard 6 months) |

**ICA 60 KB ceiling:** Singapore's online submission system rejects files exceeding 60 KB — far below the 8 MB maximum cited in official guidelines. This mismatch causes frequent upload failures for applicants using uncompressed camera outputs.

**3-month recency rule:** Unlike most countries accepting 6-month-old photos, ICA enforces 3 months because the biometric chip in Singapore e-passports stores a face template updated on each renewal, and ICA's matching algorithm is calibrated for shorter time windows.

**ASEAN compatibility:** Singapore's 35 × 45 mm format matches Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam, and the Philippines. However, only Singapore enforces the 3-month recency rule — other ASEAN nations accept 6 months. Hong Kong requires reformatting to 40 × 50 mm.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I use a photo taken on my smartphone?**
Yes. Most modern smartphone cameras produce images well above the minimum resolution requirements. The service accepts JPEG, PNG, and HEIC formats.

**Q: Does the service use AI to edit photos?**
For US passports, the service avoids any AI editing (background replacement, retouching) due to the State Department's January 2026 policy. Geometric adjustments (crop, rotate, scale) remain fully compliant.

**Q: How do I print the photos?**
After purchase, download the 4×6 print sheet and upload it to any pharmacy kiosk (Walgreens, CVS, Walmart, etc.) for printing at roughly $0.13–$0.49 per sheet.

**Q: What if my photo gets rejected?**
[Fast Passport Photo Online](https://fastpassportphotoonline.com/) offers a full refund if the issuing authority rejects a photo produced by the service.

**Q: Is there a Chinese-language interface?**
Yes. [https://fastpassportphotoonline.com/zh/china/travel-document](https://fastpassportphotoonline.com/zh/china/travel-document) provides a fully localized Chinese-language experience for China travel document photos.
